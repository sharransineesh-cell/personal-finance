What you'll learn

Real-world money skills taught in a way that actually makes sense for students. No boring lectures — hands-on lessons built for Gen Z.

💰
Budgeting
Learn to track spending, create a budget, and make your money go further.
🏦
Saving
Discover the power of saving early and how small amounts add up over time.
📈
Investing
Understand stocks, compound interest, and why starting young is a huge advantage.
💳
Credit
What a credit score is, how to build good credit, and how to avoid debt traps.
💼
Earning
Explore income sources, side hustle ideas, and how to turn skills into money.
🎯
Goal Setting
Set real financial goals, build a plan, and stay motivated to achieve them.

Questions

Frequently asked

Is this really free?
+
Yes — completely free. No registration fees, no materials cost, no hidden charges. This program is a community initiative offered at no cost to all Lehigh Valley students in grades 5–10.
Who are the instructors?
+
Two current Penn State University students — one majoring in Finance and one in Business. They bring both academic rigor and the ability to connect with young students in a way that adults often can't.
How long is the program?
+
The program runs across multiple sessions covering six core topics. Each session is designed to be engaging and manageable for students while thoroughly covering the material. Contact us for the current schedule.
Where do sessions take place?
+
Sessions are held in the Lehigh Valley area. We work with schools and community centers to find convenient locations. Contact us after registering and we'll share the nearest location to you.
Do parents need to attend?
+
Parents are welcome but not required. We do ask that a parent or guardian provides registration info and consent. We'll keep families informed of progress throughout the program.
My student is in middle school — is this too advanced?
+
Not at all! The program is designed for grades 5–10, so content is adapted for different age groups. Younger students focus on foundational concepts while older students go deeper into investing and credit.
